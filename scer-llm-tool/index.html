Environmental Impact

The SCER rating helps organizations make sustainable AI choices by providing transparent, comparable metrics on carbon efficiency. By selecting higher-rated models, organizations can significantly reduce their AI-related carbon footprint while maintaining performance requirements.

Switching from a D-rated to an A-rated model can reduce carbon emissions by up to 75% for the same workload.

About SCER for LLM

The SCER (Software Carbon Efficiency Rating) for LLM tool provides transparent carbon efficiency ratings for Large Language Models, helping organizations make sustainable AI deployment decisions.

What is SCER?

SCER is a standardized framework developed by the Green Software Foundation to measure and compare the carbon efficiency of software systems. This tool applies the SCER methodology specifically to Large Language Models, providing an easy-to-understand A-E rating scale similar to energy labels on consumer appliances.

Why Carbon Efficiency Matters

AI models, particularly Large Language Models, consume significant computational resources and energy. As AI deployment scales globally, understanding and optimizing the carbon footprint of these models becomes crucial for sustainable technology development. By choosing more efficient models, organizations can reduce their environmental impact while maintaining high performance.

Data Sources

This tool aggregates data from multiple trusted sources:

Important Disclaimer

Accuracy Disclaimer: The SCER ratings and efficiency metrics displayed in this tool are based on publicly available benchmark data from third-party sources (ML.ENERGY Leaderboard, Hugging Face LLM-Perf, etc.). This data may not reflect the complete or most current picture of a model's carbon efficiency. Scores should be considered preliminary assessments and may vary based on hardware configuration, usage patterns, and measurement methodology.

Verification Required: Please independently verify all data sources, benchmark results, and efficiency claims before making decisions based on these evaluations. Model performance, energy consumption, and data availability may change over time. Always cross-reference with official model documentation and conduct your own testing where possible.

Use Case: This tool is a reference implementation of the SCER for LLM Draft Specification and is intended for educational, research, and demonstration purposes to promote transparency and awareness in sustainable AI development. This tool is not suitable for critical business decisions or production deployment choices without thorough independent verification and testing.

Data sources may have limitations in coverage, freshness, and methodology. For serious decision-making, consult multiple sources, conduct independent benchmarks, and engage with carbon efficiency experts.
